## Three review lenses (same source reviewer; no separate worker execution)

**Code compatibility:** The network, runtime, queue, media and overlay class layouts are incompatible across these histories. File-level copy/cherry-pick is inappropriate without new adapters. Current PR16 owns the portable engine and host editor; source-only comparisons above do not certify equivalent semantics.

**Bug-hunt targets:** (1) G12 host save while audio is stopped versus while a restore is pending; (2) source revision/epoch mismatch and stale checkpoint rejection; (3) PR10 broker deadline/seek/timeout under no audio callback; (4) current mapped header changed between validation and identity check; (5) fault reset, rollback and quality-change interaction under graph retirement. Each needs a red-to-green regression on **the eventual changed source**, not a narrative pass.

**Defensive boundaries:** Cross-process shared-memory layout/ABI, time-of-check/time-of-use, lifetime/reclamation, untrusted frame admission, no spinning/allocating/locking on audio, and hostile checkpoint sizes must be tested after adaptation. Existing source says same-user mappings and matching ABI; neither local synthetic tests nor old PR claims establish hostile peer or arbitrary-host safety.
